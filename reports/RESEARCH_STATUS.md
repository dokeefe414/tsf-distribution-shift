# Research status and advisor-aligned roadmap

**Updated:** October 20, 2026  
**Study:** forecasting under distribution shift, with emphasis on perturbation response.

## Historical work already in this repository

1. A synthetic Gaussian latent process with a controlled shift, observed through a nonlinear transform: generated by **code/generate_data.py**.
2. A more complex three-regime generator with nonlinear lag dependence, changing seasonality, time-varying variance, spikes and trends: **code/generate_complex_data.py**.
3. Rolling ARIMA and fixed/adaptive Kalman comparisons: **code/arima_complex.py**, **code/model_comparison.py**, and **kalman/code/**. This early comparison is useful exploratory work, **not a finalized fair benchmark**. In particular, the early ARIMA plot includes copied training observations and the adaptive KF can interpret regime shifts as increased measurement noise.
4. A new *stationary* Gaussian AR(1) dataset, custom attention-based Transformer, one-step train/validation/test forecasting, and Gaussian input perturbation experiments: **code/generate_perturbation_data.py**, **code/transformer_model.py**, and **code/perturbation_experiment.py**.
5. A training-fitted linear AR(1) comparison on exactly the same test windows and paired perturbations: **code/linear_baseline_comparison.py**.
